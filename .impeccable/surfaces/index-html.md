## Scope
index.html — the F-16 exploded-view showcase. Visitor mode: Experience. Audience: portfolio visitors. Job: watch the airframe come apart and back together; orbit it; learn part names. Content: the model and its ten part labels only; credit amf1re.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is the jet's head-up display. The interface is collimated symbology floating over the aircraft, not a control panel beside it. Refuses the category default: a dark card with a slider bottom-centre and a spinning model.
OWN-WORLD: Dusk sky ground (deep slate #0c1218 blending to a thin warm horizon band), HUD phosphor green (#6CFF8F) used only for symbology strokes and type, no fills, no boxes except target-designation brackets; ghost-dim green (#6CFF8F at 22%) for inactive ticks. Face: B612 / B612 Mono (Airbus cockpit display face). 1px strokes, no glow bloom.
STORY: Visitor sees the jet banking slowly in dusk light under a HUD; hits "DISASSEMBLE" (or scrubs the tape) and watches sections peel away in sequence; hovers and a designation bracket locks on a part with its name.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed canvas. Top-left: "F-16A" + "AIRFRAME / EXPLODED VIEW" as HUD caption. Top-right: mode switch "AIRFRAME · COCKPIT(soon)". Right edge: vertical explode tape (0–100 ticks, caret = t) like an altitude tape — the scrubber. Bottom-centre: the primary action ASSEMBLE/DISASSEMBLE as bracketed HUD soft-key. Bottom-left: SEP readout "SEP 000%" and part count "10 SECTIONS". Bottom-right: credit.
FORM: HUD symbology (grounded list position 3 of 7; assigned). Seed key b59760f2. Raise from declined seven-segment challenger: unlit ghost digits in every numeric readout. Raise from declined cracktro challenger: no enclosing rules or panels anywhere; everything rides baselines.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
